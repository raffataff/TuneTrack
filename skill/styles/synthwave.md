---

## producer
- 90-118 bpm (or 80-100 for darker ones), Aeolian or Dorian. 16-bar sections; the chorus-like B opens up with
  the lead and bigger drums.
- The band: [supersaw] or analogue pad, [analog_bass] in straight 8ths, a lead synth, arps, a drum machine with a
  gated snare. No acoustic instruments except maybe an electric guitar or sax solo.

## composer
- Minor-key progressions that feel nostalgic: i-bVI-bIII-bVII, i-bVII-bVI-bVII, vi-IV-I-V in the relative major.
  One chord a bar, held.
- The lead sings: long notes, big leaps up to the climax, a hook that repeats with a variation each time.
- An arp line (pattern() in 16ths, "1 5 8 5" or "1 3 5 8 10 8 5 3") keeps the motion under held pads.
- Everything quantised.

## arranger
- Pad wide and sustained; bass in 8ths on the root; arp in 16ths an octave above the pad; lead on top.
- Layer the lead an octave up in the chorus; drop the arp out of the verse to make room.
- Stay on the grid; velocity steps, not humanising.

## rhythm
- Drum machine: kick on 1 and 3 (or four on the floor for faster cuts), a big gated snare on 2 and 4, straight 8th
  or 16th hats, a tom fill into each section.
- Bass: straight 8ths on the root, an octave jump on the last 8th of a bar; locked to the kick, quantised.

## sound
- [supersaw] or a big analogue pad, [analog_bass], a saw or square lead with slow vibrato, [saw_pluck] for arps, a
  drum machine kit or [drums]. Slow filter attacks on the pads.

## mix
- Wide: pads and arps spread, bass and kick centre, the lead centre and on top.
- Big reverb on the snare (gated feel: long and cut off) and lead; a tempo-synced delay (dotted 8th) on the lead
  and arp.
- Sidechain the pad and bass from the kick (4-6 dB) for the pulse. SSL-style EQ on drums and bass, a gentle top
  shelf on pads. Master -14 to -16 LUFS.

## review
- The snare and the lead define the style: a small snare or a lead that never soars is a fault.
- Out-of-style: acoustic drums, swing, jazz chords, a dry mix.
- Check the pump: pads that do not breathe with the kick sound static here.
