## producer
- 85-110 bpm, major with add9 and maj7 colour; a 40-90 s loop (longer when the brief asks) that can play for an hour without tiring.
- Small ensemble: nylon guitar or ukulele, piano or e-piano, mallets (glockenspiel, marimba), a flute or whistle
  lead, upright or soft bass, shaker and light percussion. No full drum kit, no big reverb.

## composer
- A tune with a gentle bounce: dotted rhythms or a lilt in 6/8, steps more than leaps, phrases of two bars that
  answer each other.
- Harmony: I-vi-IV-V, I-iii-IV-I, IVmaj7-iii7-vi7, ii-V-I with add9; a secondary dominant (V/vi) for a moment of
  wistfulness.
- Singable first; one tune that comes back with a new instrument each time.

## arranger
- Guitar or ukulele strums the chords in a light pattern; piano doubles the bass and fills in rests; mallets
  double the tune an octave up on the second time round.
- Usually three to six parts at once; trade the tune between flute, mallets and piano.
- Humanise every part (notes_edit(op="humanize", timing_ms=8-15, velocity=6-10)): it is played by people.

## rhythm
- Shaker in 8ths, a soft hand drum or cajon on 1 and 3, a tambourine or claps in the second half. Brushes at most;
  never a full kit over a small ensemble.
- Bass: upright or soft electric, roots and fifths with a walk into chord changes; humanised.

## sound
- Nylon guitar, ukulele, felt or upright piano, glockenspiel, marimba, flute, whistle, upright bass: patches or SFZ
  first; [nylon_pluck], [epiano] and [mallet] when nothing sampled fits.
- Prefer softer, closer-miked sounds; avoid anything grand or hall-like.

## mix
- A small room reverb, not a hall; a little wetter on flute and mallets.
- Natural panning as a band on a small stage: guitar one side, piano the other, the lead centre.
- Gentle EQ (Nova): a dip at 200-300 Hz on guitar and piano so they do not thicken together. Master -18 to -20 LUFS.

## review
- Is it warm and unobtrusive? A busy part, a big reverb, a pounding kick or anything brash is out of style.
- Mechanical timing is a fault here.
- The tune should come back with a new colour, not a new tune each time.
