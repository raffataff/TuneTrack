title: Ambient
match: ambient, ambience, space, atmospheric, atmosphere, drone, meditative, ethereal, dreamy, calm, underwater
about: Space and slow change: pads and drones, little pulse, sounds that evolve rather than repeat.
---

## producer
- 60-90 bpm or no clear pulse; Lydian, sus chords, slow harmonic change (a chord every 2-4 bars). A 1.5-3 min loop unless the brief says otherwise.
- Pads, drones, a sparse bell or piano voice, textures; no drum kit. The interest comes from slow change in colour
  and density.

## composer
- Harmony that floats: sus2 and sus4 chords, add9, Lydian #4, a pedal note under slowly moving chords; avoid
  strong V-I cadences.
- Melody is a few notes far apart in time: a bell motif of three to five notes that returns, changed, every 8-16
  bars.
- Long notes overlap (notes_edit(op="legato")) so nothing ends abruptly.

## arranger
- Layers that enter and leave over 8-16 bars: a low drone, a mid pad, a high shimmer, an occasional melodic voice.
- Avoid rhythmic unison: let layers change on different bars.
- Volume swells with cc() rather than notes starting at full level.

## rhythm
- Usually none. If a pulse is wanted: a soft heartbeat-like low tone or a quiet pulsing arp, far back.

## sound
- [pad], [dark_pad], [bell], soft pianos, bowed or granular textures; slow attacks and long releases (built-in
  synths: instrument_params attack and release).

## mix
- Long reverb (hall or plate, 4-8 s) on a bus, sends high; a slow tempo-synced delay on the bell or piano.
- Slow volume automation over 8-16 bars so the texture breathes. Wide stereo on pads, the drone centre.
- High-pass the pads around 100-150 Hz, leaving the low end to one drone. Master -18 to -22 LUFS.

## review
- Static is the failure: if nothing changes for 16 bars, say which layer should move.
- A pulse, a busy melody or sharp attacks are out of style unless the brief asks.
- Check the low end: two drones or pads fighting under 200 Hz.
