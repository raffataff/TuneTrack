---
title: Horror and tension
match: horror, tension, suspense, creepy, dread, scary, eerie, thriller, haunted, survival horror, dark
about: Dread built from clusters, drones and silence; the music withholds more than it plays.
---

## producer
- 50-80 bpm or no pulse at all; clusters, tritones, pedals. Long stretches of near silence are part of the form.
- A drone bed, sparse high sounds, low pulses; stingers for scares. Plan where the music drops out entirely.

## composer
- Harmony: minor seconds and tritones, chromatic clusters, a minor chord with a flat 2 or a major 7 on top, a
  pedal that the chords move against. No cadences that resolve.
- Melody, if any: a fragment of two or three notes, a falling half step, a lullaby-like motif played wrong.
- Leave silence: bars with nothing but the drone are the tension.

## arranger
- Extreme registers: very low drones, very high sustained notes, nothing comfortable in the middle.
- Slow crescendos with cc() over many bars, cut off suddenly.
- Humanise lightly; irregular timing on the sparse events helps.

## rhythm
- No beat. A low pulse like a heartbeat that speeds up as tension rises; single low hits; scraping or metallic
  sounds as events.

## sound
- [dark_pad] for drones, a sparse high [bell] or detuned music box, sampled strings for clusters and tremolo,
  bowed metal or noise textures.

## mix
- Wide and deep: long dark reverb, the drone very low and wide, sparse events placed far left or right.
- Dynamics wide: quiet passages near silence, stingers loud. Do not compress it flat. Master -20 to -24 LUFS.
- A low-pass on the drone so it is felt rather than heard.

## review
- Too much music is the usual fault: a beat, a tune, or no silence.
- Resolving cadences or comfortable consonance break the dread.
- Check the stingers land: loud enough against the quiet, and on the hits.
