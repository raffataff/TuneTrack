about: Club energy: four on the floor or a breakbeat, a bass that moves with the kick, builds and drops.
---

## producer
- 120-150 bpm (house 120-128, trance 136-140, drum and bass 170-175 halftime), minor. Sections in 8 or 16 bars:
  intro, build, drop, break, drop; a game loop is build into drop and back.
- Kick, bass, a lead or chord stab, arps, a pad for the break, risers and impacts for transitions.

## composer
- A hook of one or two bars that the drop is built on; harmony of three or four chords (i-bVI-bIII-bVII,
  i-bVII-bVI-v), one per bar or two.
- Chord stabs or plucks on off-beats and syncopations; a lead with a repeated rhythmic cell.
- Everything quantised; velocity in steps for accents.

## arranger
- The drop: kick, bass, hook, drums at full; the break: pad and lead only, no kick; the build: snare rolls doubling
  in speed, a filter opening, a riser.
- Layer the hook (a pluck plus a saw an octave up); keep the low end to kick and bass alone.
- Stay on the grid.

## rhythm
- House and techno: kick on every beat, clap or snare on 2 and 4, open hat on the off-beats, 16th closed hats.
  Drum and bass: a breakbeat with the snare on 2 and 4 at half time. A fill or snare roll in the last bar of each 8.
- Bass: off-beat 8ths (house), a rolling 16th line, or long notes that duck under the kick; locked to the kick.

## sound
- A punchy kick (a kit patch or [drums]), [reese_bass] or [analog_bass], [saw_pluck] for arps and stabs,
  [supersaw] for the lead and chords, [pad] for the break.

## mix
- Kick and bass own the low end: high-pass everything else around 150 Hz; sidechain bass and pads from the kick
  (fx_add("Bass", "builtin:compressor", sidechain="Kick")), 4-8 dB of pumping on pads.
- Tempo-synced delays and a short plate on leads and claps; the drop wide, the break wider.
- SSL-style EQ and compression on drums and bass. Master -12 to -14 LUFS for action; loud but not crushed.

## review
- The drop has to hit harder than the build: level, low end and width. Name what is missing.
- Mud: bass and kick fighting, or a pad with low end left in.
- Out of style: swing, humanised timing, acoustic drums.
